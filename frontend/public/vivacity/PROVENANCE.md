`isabella-scene-poster.webp` is a 26 KB first-paint fallback captured from the
Factory WebGL renderer at a recorded MuJoCo state in `src/generated/labOpening.json`.
It is removed from view after the interactive scene finishes loading. It is
presentation artwork, never a live experiment frame or evidence returned by the
runtime. The homepage's plotted positions and delivery counts come from the
recorded fixture; the laboratory's results come from new backend runs.
