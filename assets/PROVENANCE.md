# Panda model provenance

Source: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/0059d4335f8156206f63a35662313385f7ad6d74/franka_emika_panda

Retrieved during implementation, 2026-10-10. Revision: `0059d4335f8156206f63a35662313385f7ad6d74`.

The vendored `panda.xml`, mesh assets, and Apache-2.0 LICENSE are preserved. Runtime scene additions are in `vivacity/robotics.py`: table, ground, blue fixed target, obstacle, fingertip site and 2 ms timestep. The Menagerie collision meshes, inertial properties, joints and position actuators remain intact. Rendering meshes are included; no rendering or pretrained video is required for computation.
